Authorities are struggling to understand a sudden, massive shift in global geopolitical realities following an undocumented temporal event. Reports indicate the timeline has been fundamentally altered, seemingly as a result of the metahuman known as "The Flash" interfering with past events to prevent a personal tragedy. This temporal fracture has resulted in an unrecognizable, dystopian present: Atlantis and Themyscira are currently engaged in a devastating world war, Bruce Wayne is dead while his father operates as a much more brutal Batman, and Superman is a captive of the United States government. The stability of this fragile new reality remains highly questionable.
