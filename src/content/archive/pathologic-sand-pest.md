A complete quarantine has been enforced around the isolated Town-on-Gorkhon following the outbreak of a highly virulent and localized pathogen known as the "Sand Pest." Health officials and military personnel have established an impenetrable perimeter as the mortality rate inside the settlement approaches catastrophic levels. The disease, characterized by severe neurological degradation and rapid physical necrosis, has completely baffled both metropolitan medical specialists and the region's indigenous healers. Reports filtering out of the quarantine zone suggest a complete breakdown of civil order, with factions fighting bitterly over dwindling supplies and experimental cures.
