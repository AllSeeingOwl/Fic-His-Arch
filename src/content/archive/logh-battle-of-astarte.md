Disaster has struck the Free Planets Alliance in the Astarte Starzone. Initial casualty reports from the frontline describe a complete tactical breakdown of the Alliance's expeditionary forces. Rather than engaging the combined Alliance fleets in a unified confrontation, Imperial High Commander Reinhard von Lohengramm executed a daring divide-and-conquer maneuver, systematically destroying the 4th and 6th Fleets before they could consolidate. The 2nd Fleet barely managed a strategic withdrawal, saved only by the sudden tactical intervention of Commodore Yang Wen-li. The catastrophic loss of personnel and vessels has plunged Alliance high command into crisis.
