The United States government has formally renounced the floating metropolis of Columbia following a series of international incidents culminating during the Boxer Rebellion. Conceived as a symbol of American exceptionalism and kept aloft through advanced quantum levitation, the city has instead gone rogue under the fanatical leadership of its founder, Zachary Hale Comstock. In response to federal demands to stand down, Columbia has officially seceded from the Union and retreated into the upper atmosphere, effectively vanishing from radar and public sight. Rumors persist of strange chronological "tears" opening in the city's wake, suggesting a troubling mastery over localized spacetime.
