Neo-Tokyo lies in ruins once again following an apocalyptic singularity event triggered in the city's olympic sector. Government sources have refused to officially comment, but witnesses report a localized, rapidly expanding sphere of destructive energy that systematically vaporized everything in its path, echoing the devastating blast that destroyed the original Tokyo decades earlier. The event was reportedly catalyzed by a rogue military esper project involving a teenage biker named Tetsuo Shima, whose uncontrolled psychic mutations ultimately outstripped the military's containment protocols. The structural integrity of the entire region has collapsed, leaving survivors to navigate a chaotic, newly formed wasteland.
