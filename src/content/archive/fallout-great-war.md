In a devastating escalation of the ongoing Sino-American War, a massive, unprovoked barrage of nuclear warheads was detected early this morning, plunging the globe into what historians will undoubtedly record as "The Great War." Over the span of just two hours, the sheer volume of atomic detonations has plunged major population centers across North America and Asia into radioactive ruin. As the initial blasts subside, select citizens are seeking refuge in subterranean "Vaults" constructed by the Vault-Tec Corporation. With global communications completely severed and nuclear winter imminent, the survival of the human species is now gravely in doubt.
