Unsettling astrophysical data suggests that our local solar system is trapped in a localized, self-sustaining temporal loop of exactly 22 minutes duration. The loop systematically resets at the exact moment the local star goes supernova, incinerating the surrounding planets. However, specialized memory-recording technology, purportedly reverse-engineered from ruins left by an ancient civilization known as the Nomai, is allowing at least one local astronaut to retain knowledge across the temporal resets. This individual remains the system's only hope for unraveling the mysteries of the "Eye of the Universe" before the macroscopic universe succumbs entirely to heat death.
