Unprecedented reports from the European front indicate a massive, unprovoked mobilization of Soviet Union forces crossing into Western Europe. In a timeline drastically altered by the sudden and unexplained disappearance of Adolf Hitler decades prior, the geopolitical landscape has destabilized in an entirely unexpected direction. Military analysts in London express grave concern over the rapid deployment of heavy Soviet armor, while allied forces scramble to establish a defensive perimeter against what appears to be a full-scale continental invasion orchestrated by Joseph Stalin.
