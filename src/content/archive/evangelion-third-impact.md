Following a massive and highly classified military engagement in the geofront beneath Tokyo-3, satellite telemetry confirms the initiation of an apocalyptic event designated as "Third Impact." Global communication networks are collapsing amidst reports of widespread physical anomalies affecting the human population. Whistleblowers within the special agency NERV had previously warned of a clandestine "Human Instrumentality Project" designed to force the next stage of human evolution by eliminating individual physical boundaries. With the activation of the Evangelion units and the descent of the final "Angel," the structural integrity of the biosphere is failing rapidly.
