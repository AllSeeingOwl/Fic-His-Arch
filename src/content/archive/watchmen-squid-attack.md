Emergency services in New York City are overwhelmed following an unprecedented and catastrophic event that has leveled entire city blocks. In an instant, a massive, genetically engineered biological entity—resembling a monstrous, telepathic squid—materialized in the heart of the metropolis, emitting a psychic shockwave that killed millions instantly. The creature immediately expired upon arrival, leaving behind a devastating footprint of destruction and a populace plunged into absolute terror. The unprecedented nature of the attack has allegedly forced the United States and the Soviet Union to abruptly halt their escalating Cold War tensions in order to address this terrifying new threat.
