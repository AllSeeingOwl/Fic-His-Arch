Emergency broadcasts from the highly secretive Black Mesa Research Facility in the New Mexico desert report a catastrophic failure during an anomalous materials test. The incident, internally designated as a "Resonance Cascade," has reportedly ruptured the dimensional barrier, allowing highly aggressive extraterrestrial biological entities from a borderworld known as "Xen" to teleport directly into the facility. The United States military's Hazardous Environment Combat Unit (HECU) has been deployed, ostensibly to contain the threat, though fleeing scientists claim the military is systematically liquidating all surviving personnel to cover up the incident. The global ramifications of the rift remain terrifyingly unclear.
