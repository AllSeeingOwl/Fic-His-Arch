A tense standoff between Earth and the approaching Trisolaran fleet has culminated in an unprecedented display of brinkmanship. Following the complete failure of Earth's space fleet against a single Trisolaran "droplet" probe, Wallfacer Luo Ji has successfully implemented a cosmic "dead hand" system. By utilizing the sun as an amplifier, Ji has rigged a transmission array capable of broadcasting the exact gravitational coordinates of both Earth and Trisolaris to the greater universe—a direct invocation of "dark forest" deterrence that guarantees the destruction of both civilizations by predatory alien species. The Trisolaran fleet has been forced to halt its invasion, marking the tenuous beginning of what sociologists are calling the "Deterrence Era."
