Historical archivists have uncovered massive temporal anomalies surrounding the event previously designated as the "Day of Lavos." Original projections indicated that a subterranean extraterrestrial parasite would erupt from the Earth's crust in 1999 AD, reigning destruction upon the planet and pushing humanity to the brink of extinction. However, newly discovered timeline ripples suggest a small band of time-traveling insurgents, originating from the year 1000 AD, systematically disrupted the creature's life cycle across prehistoric, medieval, and future epochs. Consequently, the prophesied apocalypse appears to have been canceled, leaving scholars to debate the ethical implications of chronological interference.
