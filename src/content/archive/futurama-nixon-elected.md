In a staggering political upset, the disembodied, preserved head of former US President Richard Nixon has been elected President of Earth. Pollsters initially dismissed Nixon's campaign, which capitalized on a constitutional loophole arguing that "nobody" could serve more than two terms, and that as a mere head, he technically constituted "nobody." Following a bitterly contested campaign against opponents John Jackson and Jack Johnson, Nixon secured victory by a margin of a single vote. Transition teams are currently outfitting the new administration's headquarters with enhanced life-support tanks and an expanded robotic security detail.
