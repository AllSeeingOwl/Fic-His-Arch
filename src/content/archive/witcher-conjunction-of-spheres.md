Elven historical scholars continue to debate the far-reaching implications of the "Conjunction of the Spheres," an ancient cosmic cataclysm that forcefully merged multiple disparate realities. According to magical records, the boundaries between worlds fractured approximately 1,500 years ago, depositing myriad alien species—including ghouls, vampires, and other monstrosities—onto the Continent. Crucially, the event also facilitated the arrival of humans and introduced the primordial force of "Chaos" or magic to the realm. The prolonged ecological and societal fallout from this multidimensional collision remains the primary driver of conflict across the Continent today.
